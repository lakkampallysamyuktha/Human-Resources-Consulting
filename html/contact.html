05 / Your Context

Context is worth more than categories.

We do not ask organizations to fit themselves into a fixed size band or a maturity model. What helps us prepare is a plain description of what is happening and what the decision looks like.

There is no scoring, no benchmark and no cost to simply describing the situation in your own words.

Scale, in your own words

Mid-sized, distributed, holding structure, family-run, public sector — whatever is accurate.

Situation, in your own words

A restructure, a leadership transition, a capability gap, a cultural shift — not a category.

Urgency, in your own words

Exploring an option, considering a decision, actively weighing choices or already acting.

08 / Before We Begin

A little preparation makes the first conversation more useful.

  1. 01

    A short description of the situation

    What is happening, and why it matters now — a few sentences is enough.

  2. 02

    Who else is involved in the decision

    Whether this is a leadership question, an HR question or a shared one.

  3. 03

    What good would look like

    A clearer picture of what you would like to be different six or twelve months from now.

  4. 04

    Any constraints we should know about

    Timing, sensitivity, disclosure or governance considerations.
